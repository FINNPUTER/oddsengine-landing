FAQ
Common questions.
What does OddsEngine trade?
Two market categories: crypto-linked prediction markets (BTC, ETH, SOL Up/Down) and weather temperature markets across 35 global cities. Both focus on short-horizon markets where structural pricing inefficiencies occur regularly.
How does the weather engine work?
Our proprietary forecasting engine cross-references multiple meteorological data sources and compares the resulting consensus against live market pricing. When we detect a statistically significant divergence, the system generates a trading signal. The specific methodology and data sources are proprietary.
How does the access model work?
One-time access fee based on tier selection. Profit share is deducted automatically per resolved trade — not invoiced monthly. Starter retains 50%, Professional 70%, Premium 80%. All settlement is visible on the dashboard in real time.
Do you have custody of my funds?
No. You maintain your own wallet and private keys at all times. OddsEngine executes on your behalf but never holds, transfers, or withdraws your funds independently.
What risk controls are in place?
Position sizing relative to bankroll, maximum exposure limits, per-trade size caps, and market quality filters that reject low-liquidity setups before any execution.
How do I request access?
Join the Telegram channel for system updates and performance data. When ready, DM for onboarding — includes wallet setup, platform configuration, and access provisioning.
What's the difference between the tiers?
All tiers include the same platform, execution, and dashboard. The only difference is the profit split. Higher tier = you keep a larger share of every winning trade.
Why is access capped at 25?
Execution quality is inversely correlated with participant count. Each additional account increases orderbook impact and reduces fill rates. 25 is the current ceiling for maintaining system-level performance guarantees.
What are the risks?
Trading prediction markets involves risk of loss. Performance varies with market conditions, liquidity, and timing. OddsEngine provides trading infrastructure — not financial advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
