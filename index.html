FAQ
Common questions.
What does OddsEngine trade?
Crypto-linked prediction markets on Polymarket — specifically short-horizon markets tied to BTC, ETH, SOL, and XRP price movements across 5-minute, 15-minute, and hourly windows.
How are signals generated?
A 21-indicator scoring engine compares real-time crypto prices from Coinbase and Binance against Polymarket orderbook data. Each market window receives a composite score. Only signals above the configured threshold are acted on.
Do you have custody of my funds?
No. OddsEngine operates on a self-custody model. You maintain your own Polymarket wallet and private keys. The platform generates signals and executes on your behalf, but never holds or transfers funds independently.
What risk controls are in place?
Position sizing relative to bankroll, configurable stop-loss levels, maximum simultaneous exposure limits, per-trade size caps, and market quality filters that reject illiquid or wide-spread markets before execution.
What infrastructure is required?
OddsEngine runs on a dedicated VPS with WebSocket connections to Coinbase, Binance, and Polymarket. Signals and execution are delivered via Telegram. You need a funded Polymarket wallet on Polygon.
What are the risks?
Trading prediction markets involves risk of loss. Signal quality varies with market conditions, liquidity, and execution timing. OddsEngine provides trading infrastructure — not financial advice. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
