How the number is made

How it works

Polymarket runs temperature markets on 50 cities. Eleven brackets, one wins. We forecast the whole distribution and compare it to what the market is actually charging. Where the gap is worth taking, we post.

Market discovery

Every live market, automatically

No fixed city list. The engine reads the market feed and extracts the brackets, the unit, the deadline and the resolution rules, including which station the market actually settles on. New cities appear the day they list.

Probability engine

A probability for every bracket

We forecast the day's high at the exact point the market settles on, not the city centre, which can be a full bracket away. Every bracket gets a probability. They sum to 100%.

The real price

The price you actually pay

The displayed percentage is not a price you can fill. We check what is really available, and in what size. If there is not enough, there is no signal.

How we place it

One quote, whichever lands

A few neighbouring brackets, split so the payout is identical whichever one is the day's high. One number to read, one outcome to check.

Most markets produce nothing. The engine stays silent when the edge is thin, the market is too small, the spread is too wide, or the models disagree. A channel posting twenty signals a day is posting fifteen bad ones.

Robustness

The edge doesn't move

We swept the model's confidence setting across its entire plausible range. Hit rate swung from 77% to 96%. The edge over the market barely moved. An overfit wanders with its parameters. This doesn't.

Control

Buying blind loses 38%

Same markets, same period, no engine, just buy the cheapest brackets every day: −38.1%. The extremes are expensive and the middle is cheap. That gap is what the engine is built to find.

Correction

The correction nobody else makes

Picking the best of many blocks selects for your own optimism. That is measurable, so we measure it and subtract it before a signal goes out. Otherwise you promise 81 and deliver 72. Fewer signals, and every one of them means what it says.
