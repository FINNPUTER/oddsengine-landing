Setup Guide

Get connected in
under 10 minutes.

Follow these steps to connect your wallet to OddsEngine. Once complete, the system begins trading on your account automatically.

01 Create a Polymarket account

Go to Polymarket and create an account. This is the prediction market platform where OddsEngine executes trades.

Go to
https://polymarket.com
Sign up with email or connect an existing wallet. If you're new, email signup is the fastest option.
02 Set up your wallet

You need a Polygon-compatible wallet to hold your funds. We recommend Phantom — it supports Polygon, is easy to use, and works on mobile and desktop.

Download Phantom
https://phantom.app
Install Phantom → Create or import a wallet → Switch to Polygon network.
Important
Create a new, dedicated wallet only for OddsEngine. Do not use your primary wallet. This keeps your funds separated and your main wallet secure.
03 Fund your wallet with USDC

Transfer USDC to your Polygon wallet. This is your trading bankroll — the system trades with these funds. You maintain full custody at all times.

Requirements
Token: USDC (or USDC.e) on Polygon network
Amount: Your chosen bankroll — the system adapts position sizing automatically
Gas: Keep a small amount of POL (Polygon) for transaction fees (~$1 worth is sufficient)
Network
Make sure you send USDC on the Polygon network. Sending on Ethereum mainnet will result in funds on the wrong chain.
04 Deposit on Polymarket

Once your wallet is funded, deposit USDC into your Polymarket account. This makes the funds available for trading.

How to deposit
1. Go to polymarket.com and connect your wallet
2. Click "Deposit" in the top right
3. Enter the amount you want to deposit
4. Approve and confirm the transaction in your wallet
05 Generate your API Key

Polymarket allows you to create API keys that grant trade-only access. This key lets OddsEngine execute trades on your account — without any ability to withdraw or transfer your funds.

Steps
1. Go to polymarket.com → Profile → Settings
2. Navigate to API Keys
3. Click "Create API Key"
4. Copy the API Key and API Secret
5. Send both to OddsEngine via Telegram DM (encrypted)
Security
Your private key stays with you. You never share your wallet's private key or seed phrase with anyone — including OddsEngine. The API key only grants trade permission. You can revoke it at any time from your Polymarket settings.
06 Confirmation

Once we receive your API key, OddsEngine configures your account and begins trading within 24 hours. You'll receive a confirmation via Telegram.

What happens next
The system starts trading on your account automatically. You can monitor every trade on your dashboard in real time. Profit share is calculated per trade — fully transparent, fully auditable.

Common Questions
Can OddsEngine withdraw my funds?
No. The API key only grants trade permission. Withdrawals require your private key, which you never share. You can revoke the API key at any time.
Can I withdraw my funds at any time?
Yes. Your wallet, your keys. You can withdraw from Polymarket back to your wallet at any time. Note that open positions may need to resolve first.
How do I stop OddsEngine from trading?
Revoke your API key in Polymarket settings. The system loses trade access immediately. You can also DM us on Telegram to pause your account.
How is the profit share calculated?
After each profitable trade, the system calculates your share based on your tier. The split is logged on your dashboard. Settlement details are visible in real time.
What if I need help during setup?
DM us on Telegram. We'll walk you through any step in real time.
